FAQ

How The Jammers works

Everything you need: how to join in, what line-up roles mean, and how to send feedback to the team.

Quick start

Join without breaking the line-up

1
Look first

Open the current setlist, see open seats, close the real gaps before bringing new songs.

2
Sign up honestly

Only take parts you can actually deliver. The line-up stays reliable that way.

3
Ask in advance

Question? Drop it in feedback early, not at the deadline.

Need help?

What happens after feedback

Your message lands in the team's Telegram immediately, so product bugs, unclear rules and gig-specific questions reach the people who can fix them.

The best feedback names the gig, the song, and the exact moment things felt off.

Participation rules

How it works

  • ●Check the current setlist, already-claimed songs and open seats.
  • ●If you see your role — claim it right away.
  • ●If the song isn't there yet, propose it only after searching.
  • ●If the gig is closed, optional positions can still be requested via the song's author.

Things to remember

  • ●Only sign up for parts you can actually close.
  • ●If your plans change, free the slot as early as possible.
  • ●Respect the final set and the song author's calls on optional positions.
Line-up technical details

What roles in the setlist mean

  • Required — needed for the song to count as ready.
  • OPT / Optional — adds colour and energy but doesn't block readiness.
  • Playback — adds context to a song but isn't a live part.

Before you sign up

  • ●Double-check the key, playback, author's notes and the rest of the setlist.
  • ●If you want to invite someone, use the invite button on the relevant cell.
